# Product Vision — Maharty AI

## The promise
Maharty is an automotive workbench, not a collection of disconnected AI buttons. A user describes an outcome in ordinary language; the system clarifies only essential ambiguity, proposes a plan, selects approved skills, executes steps, and presents reviewable results.

## Audiences
- Individual vehicle owners and sellers.
- Dealers, showrooms, and small automotive businesses.
- Teams that manage inventory, advertising, media, and customer enquiries.

## Jobs to be done
- Prepare honest, attractive vehicle photos for listings.
- Create accurate Arabic and English vehicle advertisements.
- Organize vehicle details, media, and listing status.
- Produce shareable vehicle/showroom pages.
- Create short marketing videos from supplied assets.
- Coordinate repetitive tasks through specialist assistants.

## Experience modes
- **Quick mode:** one clear task, sensible defaults, guided upload, understandable result.
- **Professional mode:** control over crop, background, output sizes, brand assets, masking, quality checks, and export settings.

## Trust principles
- Keep uploaded originals immutable.
- Clearly distinguish generated/edited media from originals.
- Never invent vehicle specifications, mileage, ownership, inspection results, or condition.
- Do not remove or conceal material damage in a way that deceives a buyer.
- Require explicit approval before publishing, sending messages, spending money, or changing connected accounts.
- Show users when a task uses a paid external provider.

## Product success measures
- Time from task request to usable result.
- Successful job completion rate.
- Human correction/rejection rate.
- Mobile task completion rate.
- Repeat usage by individual and business workspaces.
- Cost per completed task and gross margin by capability.
