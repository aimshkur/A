# Arabic RTL UX and Design Direction

## Visual direction
- Premium, calm, light-first interface; high clarity over decorative effects.
- Arabic-first RTL layout with carefully tested mixed Arabic/English numbers and labels.
- Mobile-first layouts; desktop expands workspace density without changing task logic.
- Avoid clutter, excessive gradients, parallax, ornamental 3D objects, and ambiguous icons.
- Real vehicle imagery and meaningful before/after comparisons are preferred to generic illustrations.

## Home screen
- One prominent task composer: "إيش تبغى تسوي بسيارتك؟"
- Suggested task chips with explicit outcomes, e.g. "جهّز صور السيارة للإعلان" and "اكتب إعلاناً من بيانات السيارة".
- Recent vehicles/projects and clear continue actions.
- Explain what each assistant does in plain language; avoid abstract persona names.

## Studio
- Upload area with camera/gallery affordance.
- Original and result comparison.
- Output layout options: one image, two per row, or three per row where viewport allows.
- Clear Quick / Professional mode distinction.
- Settings hidden behind understandable controls; preview before expensive processing.
- Persistent save/export actions and visible job status.

## Accessibility
- WCAG-minded contrast, focus states, semantic labels and keyboard navigation.
- Touch targets sized for mobile.
- Do not rely on color alone for status.
- Respect reduced motion and screen-reader labels.
- Test common iOS and Android viewport widths.

## Content
- Saudi-friendly Arabic language, concise instructions and familiar automotive terms.
- Never use fake ratings, fake reviews, or invented vehicle facts in demo content.
